Portable URDF and MuJoCo descriptions for PAR6 and PAROL6, with six tool
configurations per robot:

- Flange only.
- MSG with Solid jaws or Soft TPU jaws.
- SSG48 with Solid jaws or Fin-ray jaws.
- Pneumatic gripper with coupled opening/closing fingers.

Download the ZIP for your robot and simulator. Each package includes its own
meshes, physical parameters, DH tables, dimensioned kinematic review sheets,
and documentation. The PDF booklet compares all twelve configurations.
`SHA256SUMS` verifies the downloads; `release_manifest.json` identifies the
source commit and package versions.

Both arms use the corrected 177 mm forearm and common public joint axes/J6
orientation. PAROL6 uses the shorter 50.5 mm flange reach, with shared PAR6
local mass properties and the user-measured flange/MSG/SSG48 masses.

This version corrects SSG48 URDF internal rack collision hulls and the
short-jaw grasp fixture's initial object/housing overlap. All 16 electric
gripper grasp cases and eight full-stroke contact sweeps pass. The model suite
contains 91 model checks and three release-asset checks.

Motor rotor inertias, tool COM/tensors, finite drive settings and grasp
friction/force remain uncalibrated estimates. The pneumatic assembly retains
an unverified 195 g total. These packages provide consistent description and
simulation models; hardware calibration remains follow-up work.
